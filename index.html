Questions

What people ask before they sign.

It's AI. Is it going to write nonsense to my clients?

Nothing reaches a client without your approval, until you decide otherwise after watching the drafts for a few weeks. The model writes, you keep control. And for everything that does not need writing, we use plain automation, which costs less and does not get it wrong.

We already have software. We are not replacing everything.

There is no migration. The assistant plugs into what you already use, your quoting software, your inbox, your spreadsheets. It fills the gaps your tools leave rather than competing with them.

Where does our data go?

It stays in your tools and your accounts. If something has to leave your systems, we tell you what, to which provider and why, before it is set up.

Nobody here can maintain this.

That is true of most companies we work with, and it is planned for. Documentation is delivered, the people who will use it are trained, and the monthly option exists if you would rather manage none of it.

Why not a day rate?

Because on a day rate you pay for how slow we are. With a fixed price you know the cost before you commit, and any overrun is ours.

Who do you work with?

Companies of ten to sixty people with no developer in house, running on spreadsheets and copy and paste. The industry matters far less than how you work.
